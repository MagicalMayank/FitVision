FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find answers about form tracking, AI nutrition, and privacy.

How does real-time form tracking work? expand_more
FitVision uses YOLO11n-Pose neural networks running locally in your browser. It detects key joint keypoints to compute knee angles, torso lean, and range of motion (ROM) in real time without sending video frames off your device.
Is my camera video recorded or saved? expand_more
No, absolute privacy is guaranteed. Video frames are processed in memory on your device only during active workout sessions and are immediately discarded. No video recordings leave your phone or computer.
How do I change my fitness goals or body metrics? expand_more
Navigate to the Profile tab and tap the EDIT button inside the BIO INFORMATION card. You can adjust your age, weight, height, and target fitness goal anytime.
Can I switch the Voice Coach language to Hindi? expand_more
Yes! You can toggle between English and हिंदी in the top app bar of the Workout screen. The Voice Coach will speak real-time form cues and post-workout insights in your selected language.
What foods does the Nutrition Scanner recognize? expand_more
The scanner recognizes a wide variety of meals including rice dishes, rotis, paneer, chicken, dal, salads, eggs, and fruit. It estimates portion sizes and calculates macro breakdowns automatically.
How is precision percentage calculated? expand_more
Precision is derived from joint ROM stability, rep depth completion, posture alignment, and rep hysteresis smoothness evaluated during each movement cycle.
