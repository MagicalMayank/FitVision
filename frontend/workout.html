SQUAT — CORRECT FORM

  • Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly turned out
  • Lower until hips drop below knee level — don't stop at half-depth
  • Keep chest up, shoulders back — avoid rounding your lower back
  • Let knees track over your toes, don't let them cave inward
  • Keep weight in your heels/midfoot — heels stay flat on the floor

PUSH-UP — CORRECT FORM

  • Place hands flat on the floor slightly wider than shoulder-width apart
  • Extend legs straight back to balance on toes in a plank position
  • Lower your body until your chest almost touches the floor
  • Keep elbows tucked at a 45-degree angle without sagging hips
  • Push firmly through your palms to return to the starting position
